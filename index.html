Dematting

Matts, matts and more matts. How can I have my fluffy dog back?

Add-on from $15

Long and wool coated dogs are prone to getting knots in their coat and need a regular brushing routine in between their Full Groom. We understand with a busy lifestyle it can be difficult to achieve this as regularly as desired, and matts can build up in your dog's coat.

A matt is an accumulation of knots, debris and dirt that have formed into a clump of hair. Once this happens, it is more difficult and time consuming for the groomer to brush through the coat, which increases the time of the groom. If the coat is too densely matted, the only option is to shave under the matts, which means taking the haircut close to the skin.

A dematting fee may be applied on top of the grooming price depending on the condition of the mats.

Deshedding

My dog can't stop shedding!

From $55

While heavy shedding is breed specific, there is no such thing as a non-shedding dog unless it is completely hairless! Some breeds shed seasonally while others shed all year round. Your dog's health is a major factor in how much hair falls out, so if your pooch is shedding an unusual amount for their breed it is worth getting them checked by the vet.

Our De-shedding Treatment removes your dog's shedding coat which, if left unchecked and allowed to build up, will impact your dog's ability to regulate their body temperature. Loose undercoat often needs more than just combing to remove it — letting dogs leave happy and cooler without their shedded hair!

Hand Stripping

The art of removing a wire coat by hand instead of clippers.

Add-on from $30

Our groomers are experienced in the art of hand stripping. This is where a wire coated dog has their fur removed by hand as opposed to using electric clippers. It is a specialized service and only certain groomers have the skill to perform it.

Hand stripping works by removing the top layer of hair known as “guard hairs” to reveal a softer undercoat. It is a more natural form of grooming for wire coated breeds and, in the long run, gives better condition and shine to the coat as it opens up the hair follicles.

Pawdicure

Dry, cracked pads? Long nails that scratch your floors?

Walk-in from $22

  • Nail Clipping: your dog should be walking on the pads of its feet and not the toe nails. Walking on the nails creates more wear and tear on the joints. Keeping nails and pad hair short aids in arthritis prevention. Included in a standard grooming, or as a walk-in service.

  • Nail Painting: we use a “pet friendly” nail polish that is sure to show off your little darling's paws! Included in a grooming or as a walk-in service.
  • Soft Pads: for dry, cracked or irritated paw pads — a White Tea and Tea Tree detoxing foot soak followed by a Coconut Oil pad massage. Grooming add-on only.

Nail trims are walk-in: Monday to Friday 8am–5pm and Saturday 8am–3pm.

Ozone Bath Spa

All your dog's friends will be jealous. Relax! Dog life is also great!

Add-on $30 North location

This spa treatment combines three of nature's most powerful elements: heat, water and air. The Ozone Spa Bath uses hydro-massage, ozone, and aromatherapy to ease stress, bathe away physical fatigue, relax joints and muscles, and treat skin and coat.

Treatment options

  • Revitalizing: an all-round treatment to give back a sense of vitality; ideally suited to nervous or timid dogs, or those with past trauma.
  • Relaxing: a restorative treatment against tiredness and stress; great for agility, show, working dogs and active pets.
  • Circulation: reactivates blood circulation; suitable for elderly pets or those who don't get a lot of exercise.
  • Reactivating: reactivates the function of the cutis; ideal for skin conditions, heavy shedding, or restoring skin health.
  • Anti-stress: great for relaxation after a stressful situation such as moving homes or hormonal imbalances.

Benefits

  • Skin and coat: with its strong anti-bacterial function, ozone helps with a wide range of skin and hair conditions, alleviates itching and soothes hot spots. Highly recommended for heavy seasonal shedding.
  • Whole body massage: tiny yet powerful bubbles relieve tension and fatigue, relax muscles and stimulate circulation.
  • Deep cleansing: gently loosens grease and dirt from the skin, allowing skin tissues to breathe better.

The Ozone Spa Bath is offered at the North shop only.

About us.

At Oh My Dog Spa and Grooming, we believe that your furry friend deserves the best.

Our dedicated team of certified professionals treats every pet with the affection and care they would receive from royalty. From luxurious bubble baths and hair treatments to pawdicures and relaxing massages, we offer a range of grooming services designed to keep your dog looking and feeling their best.

Our commitment to your dog's safety and wellbeing

Our philosophy is simple: every dog deserves to feel safe, loved, and respected. From the moment you walk through our doors, our goal is to create an environment where pets feel comfortable and owners feel confident.

A team you can trust

We are extremely selective in choosing the people who join our team. Every staff member undergoes a careful hiring process, hands-on evaluations, and ongoing training. We do not tolerate — and would never permit — any intentional harm or mistreatment of animals.

Safety first, always

Our internal procedures and policies are designed to ensure a calm, controlled, and transparent environment. From sanitation to handling techniques to supervision, every part of our process is built with your dog's wellbeing in mind.

A stress-free, free-play environment

Dogs in our care are allowed to play freely and socialize, helping reduce stress. Crates are used only when an owner requests it, a dog shows aggression toward others, or a dog is in heat or not fixed and requires separation for safety.

Transparency you can see

Clients are always invited to observe our workspace and watch how we care for the dogs. We believe transparency builds trust, and trust is the foundation of our relationship with you and your pet.

FAQ.

Do I need an appointment for a nail trim?

No — nail trims are walk-in, Monday to Friday 8am–5pm and Saturday 8am–3pm.

How is the grooming price determined?

By your pet's breed, coat condition and the time required to complete the service. We can give an estimated range over the phone, and the most accurate quote is provided after assessing your pet in person.

Do you groom cats?

Yes. Cat Full Grooming is $120 and Cat Bath and Shed Out is $90 — both include bath, dry and nail trimming.

Where is the Ozone Bath Spa available?

The Ozone Spa Bath is offered at our North shop (216 Mistatim Road NW) only.

Is there a loyalty discount?

Yes — come back within 6 weeks and get a 10% discount.

Will my dog be kept in a crate?

Dogs in our care are allowed to play freely and socialize. Crates are used only when an owner specifically requests it, when a dog shows aggression toward others, or when a dog is in heat or not fixed and requires separation for safety.

Can I see where my dog is groomed?

Absolutely. Clients are always invited to observe our workspace and watch how we care for the dogs.

My dog's coat is matted. What happens?

A dematting fee may be applied on top of the grooming price depending on the condition of the mats. If the coat is too densely matted, the only option is to shave under the matts, close to the skin.
